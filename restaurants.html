Date night

Top romantic choices

With a view

Winthrop Arms Hotel & Restaurant

Steps away from our Airbnb. This historic spot offers a unique, old-school romantic atmosphere. While not directly "toes-in-the-sand," it sits on a hill overlooking the ocean, offering sweeping views from its dining room and seasonal porch.

winthroparms.com

Sambalos Tapas & Bar

A vibrant spot offering Spanish and Mediterranean tapas, craft cocktails, and a cozy atmosphere. Perfect for casual dinners and celebrations. One of my personal favorites!

sambalos.com
With a view

Cafe Valenciano Restaurant

Located directly across from the ocean on Winthrop Shore Drive, this intimate Italian restaurant (formerly Cafe Rossetti's) is a great local option for a date night.

cafevalenciano.com
With a view

Belle Isle Seafood

A local favorite right near Winthrop Beach; excellent seafood and pleasant views if you stroll by the water afterward. Note that this is a cafeteria-style, counter-service spot. It is fantastic for a casual lunch or sunset dinner, but it may not fit the traditional definition of "romantic" if you are looking for table service and ambiance.

90 Restaurant

Stylish, modern spot that feels perfect for a romantic dinner, with creative American dishes, great cocktails, and a cozy upscale atmosphere.

With a view

Dryft Revere

Located just north of Winthrop on Revere Beach, this is likely the closest match to a "chic, oceanfront" romantic dinner.

dryftrevere.com
With a view

ReelHouse

Located in East Boston, this spot offers some of the most stunning waterfront views of the Boston skyline.

reelhouseboston.com
