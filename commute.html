Getting around

Getting to Downtown Boston is a Breeze!

The bus stop at Crest Ave & Grovers Ave is a one-minute walk from the house. From there you have three good options.

Option 1

Hybrid commute

Park & subway. The simplest option if you have the car with you.

  1. Drive to Suffolk Downs station and park — roughly $2.50 for the day.
  2. Board the Blue Line heading Inbound / to Bowdoin.
Option 2

Classic Boston commute

Bus & subway. No car needed at all.

  1. Walk to the bus stop at Crest Ave & Grovers Ave.
  2. Take the #712 bus toward Orient Heights.
  3. Board the Blue Line at Orient Heights.
  4. You'll be downtown in about 10–12 minutes. Get off at Aquarium, Government Center, or State.
Option 3

The scenic route

Winthrop Ferry. Seasonal service only — but by far the prettiest way in.

  1. Reach the terminal by bus or Uber. There is free parking at Winthrop Landing.
  2. Sail across the harbor in 20–30 minutes.

Check the MBTA Winthrop Ferry schedule online before you set out — it changes with the season.

Good to know

Fares & payment tips

  • Pick up a CharlieCard or CharlieTicket at any subway station.
  • A single ride is about $2.40.
  • That fare includes one transfer — so the bus-to-subway hop on Option 2 is covered.

Prefer to skip the transit? An Uber or Lyft into downtown Boston runs roughly $20–$25.
